WHAT IS CURATiON?

CURATiON is a site where you can get inspiration for your next outfit, wether its a certain style you want to look for or just a fast and easy outfit to take from. Everything is here for you to copy from, just find what you like and use it.

WHAT DOES CURATION MEAN?

Curation means to choose, sort and put together something with thoughtfulness.
It is mostly used in a creative and visual context such as fashion, art, design and content. When you “Curate”, you do more than just gather things, you create a whole, a vibe, a story.
Curation in short means: deliberate selection, organization and presentation.
